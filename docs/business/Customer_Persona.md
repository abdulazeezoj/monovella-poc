# Customer Persona

## Primary Persona: Time-Constrained Care Seeker

**Working name:** The working adult with a specialist-care need

**Profile:** An adult in Nigeria who needs GP or gynecology care and is balancing work, family, and transport. They may be referred by someone they trust or already use WhatsApp and calls to find a specialist. They are willing to discover, book, pay, and follow a verified consultation through a self-service mobile journey.

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Goal | Reach a verified specialist, complete the next clinical action, and receive a clear result or outcome without losing a full workday to coordination |
| Trigger | A concern that needs GP or gynecology care, a referral, a delayed hospital appointment, or a lab request after a consultation |
| Main frustrations | Uncertain availability, long queues, repeated explanations, unclear payment or next steps, and personally chasing results |
| Current tools | Calls, WhatsApp, trusted referrers, hospital visits, paper or chat records |
| Self-service expectation | Can compare a verified provider, see price and availability, complete checkout, and track the next action without relying on a coordinator |
| Decision criteria | Trust in the provider, disclosed fee, earliest suitable slot, clear next action, and reliable handoff to a lab or pharmacy |
| Accessibility needs | Plain English, readable mobile-first screens, clear consent, visible status, and support without diagnostic claims |

## Jobs, Pains, and Gains

### Job to be done

When I need specialist care while managing work and family, help me get from a trusted consultation to the next required action so I do not have to coordinate every provider or repeat my story.

### Pains

- A hospital visit can consume a full day or more.
- A referral or unavailable clinician can break the thread of care.
- The patient must call separate providers to confirm appointments, tests, medicine availability, payment, and result return.
- Clinical records can be incomplete or inaccessible when the next provider needs them.

### Desired gains

- A verified clinician with a disclosed fee and available slot.
- One understandable journey showing what happens next and who owns it.
- A laboratory result returned to both the patient and the requesting specialist.
- Calm, non-diagnostic updates when something is delayed or needs support.

## Secondary Persona: Verified Specialist Partner

The specialist needs a self-service provider workspace for availability, correctly booked and paid patients, a structured Care Plan, and reliable access to results for laboratory requests they issued. The specialist does not need a broad clinical-record browser. Monovella must restrict access to assigned, consented journeys.

## Evidence Boundary

This persona combines the founder's concierge observations and the approved product specification. It is a research hypothesis, not a statistically validated segmentation. Interview and observe patients, specialists, laboratories, and pharmacies across the first self-service transactions before expanding the audience.
