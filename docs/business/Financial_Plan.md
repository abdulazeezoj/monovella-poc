# Financial Plan

## Decision summary

Monovella has no live platform revenue. This is a **24-month planning model**, not historical performance, an audited forecast, a tax filing, or an investor-return promise.

The ₦18.9m pre-seed ask funds the first 12 months of validation. The investor case then models a Year-2 operating scale that produces **₦18.9m cumulative pre-tax operating profit by Month 24**. That is company profit, not a dividend, equity return, or guaranteed investor payment.

The formula-driven model in [Financial_Plan.xlsx](Financial_Plan.xlsx) requires **204,707 completed paid consultations in Year 2**, or **17,059 per month**, under the 20% commission case. It reaches that volume only after Year-2 contribution covers the Year-1 validation loss, Year-2 fixed operating costs, and the ₦18.9m cumulative profit target.

## Revenue and unit economics

Patients pay the provider fee plus a disclosed platform commission. The provider receives the full provider fee. Monovella recognises commission less the modelled Nomba checkout cost.

| Consultation | Provider fee | Patient payment at 20% | Net platform revenue |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| GP | ₦4,000 | ₦4,800 | ₦720 |
| Gynecology | ₦10,000 | ₦12,000 | ₦1,800 |

The provisional mix is 82.35% GP and 17.65% gynecology. At that mix, net platform revenue is ₦910.59 and direct cost to serve is ₦240.58 per completed consultation. Contribution is **₦670.01 per consultation**. The 15% commission case remains a pricing sensitivity. Laboratory and pharmacy revenue are excluded until attachment, basket, refund, and payout evidence exists.

## Bottom-up market sizing

The market unit is a completed paid consultation. The cited Federal Ministry of Health report gives a national medical-doctor base of 66,241 and state bases of 4,609 in Lagos, 634 in Ogun, and 1,287 in FCT. It does not publish a current GP-versus-gynecology split. The workbook therefore applies the provisional planning mix until a Nigeria Health Workforce Registry export supplies specialty counts.

The mentor-provided operating ranges are 600–1,000 GP consultations per month and 250–500 gynecology consultations per month. The model applies a visible 5% Monovella-available-capacity assumption. These produce capacity bands, not demand or contracts.

```text
SOM = CEILING((Year-2 fixed costs + ABS(Year-1 loss) + profit target)
              / Year-2 contribution per consultation)

SAM/TAM = Σ(provider base × provisional specialty allocation × monthly capacity
            × 12 × Monovella-available allocation)
```

## Evidence boundary and sources

- The commission, payment-cost rate, price points, mix, direct cost, scale operating costs, and availability rate are planning inputs that must be replaced with commercial terms and live data.
- [Federal Ministry of Health State of Health of the Nation Report 2024](https://fmohconnect.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/The-State-of-Health-of-the-Nation-Report-2024.pdf) supplies the provider bases.
- The [Nigeria Health Workforce Registry](https://nhwfr.health.gov.ng/) is the replacement source for verified specialty and location counts. It requires an authorised export.
- The capacity ranges come from the mentor-provided reference image. They are not a public market study.
