# Market Research

## Market definition

Monovella measures its market in **completed paid GP and gynecology consultations**. A payment checkout is part of the consultation flow, not a separate market unit. Laboratory and pharmacy transactions remain outside the revenue model until their attachment and unit economics are measured.

## Bottom-up TAM SAM SOM

| Layer | Definition | Calculation basis |
|---|---|---|
| SOM | Year-2 consultations needed to reach ₦18.9m cumulative pre-tax company profit by Month 24 | 204,707 projected completed consultations, or 17,059/month, from the workbook formula |
| SAM | Annual capacity in Lagos, Ogun, and FCT | State medical-doctor bases × provisional GP/Gyn allocation × mentor-provided monthly capacity ranges × 12 × 5% available capacity |
| TAM | Annual national capacity | National medical-doctor base × the same capacity assumptions |

SOM is a profit-required target, not current demand, market share, signed volume, or a forecast of actual revenue. SAM and TAM are provider-capacity bands. They do not establish patient demand or conversion.

## Inputs and limitations

The Federal Ministry of Health's 2024 State of Health of the Nation Report gives a national base of 66,241 medical doctors and state bases of 4,609 in Lagos, 634 in Ogun, and 1,287 in FCT. The report does not provide a current GP-versus-gynecology split. Until an authorised Nigeria Health Workforce Registry export replaces it, the workbook transparently uses the provisional operating mix of 82.35% GP and 17.65% gynecology.

The mentor-provided capacity ranges are 600–1,000 consultations per GP per month and 250–500 consultations per gynecologist per month. The model applies a 5% Monovella-available-capacity input. This is a planning assumption, not contracted provider availability.

## Commercial translation

At the 20% planning commission, Monovella retains ₦720 per GP consultation and ₦1,800 per gynecology consultation after the modelled 10% payment-processing cost. The weighted contribution after direct service cost is ₦670.01 per completed consultation. The 15% rate is a sensitivity, not a second forecast. No laboratory or pharmacy revenue is included.
