# Lean Canvas

## Problem

Working adults seeking GP or gynecology care lose time to queues, disconnected referrals, uncertain availability, payment friction, and chasing laboratory results across providers.

## Solution

Monovella provides a patient-owned, permission-aware Care Journey from verified provider discovery and checkout through consultation, structured next action, laboratory booking, and result return.

## Customer

The initial customer is a working adult in Nigeria who can discover, book, pay for, and follow care through a self-service mobile journey. The initial provider partner needs availability control, paid bookings, structured Care Plans, and assignment-scoped clinical context.

## Revenue model

Patients pay provider fee X plus a disclosed 15% or 20% commission, capped at ₦3,000. The provider receives X. The 20% planning case produces ₦720 net platform revenue per GP consultation and ₦1,800 per gynecology consultation after the modelled payment cost. Laboratory and pharmacy revenue are excluded until measured.

## Profit and market model

Monovella has no live platform revenue. The ₦18.9m ask funds 12 months of validation. The 24-month company-profit projection targets ₦18.9m cumulative pre-tax operating profit by Month 24. Its SOM is 204,707 completed consultations in Year 2, calculated from fixed costs, the Year-1 validation loss, target profit, and contribution per consultation.

SAM and TAM are bottom-up annual provider-capacity bands, not population adoption rates. They use cited medical-doctor bases, mentor-provided GP and gynecology monthly volumes, a provisional specialty allocation, and a visible available-capacity assumption.

## Key metrics

- Completed paid consultations
- Payment success and attendance
- Provider availability and slot confirmation
- Next-action completion and result-return reliability
- Direct cost and contribution per completed consultation

## Evidence boundary

Three manually coordinated paid journeys are early evidence only. Pricing, conversion, capacity, acquisition cost, retention, and profitability remain planning assumptions until live data and commercial terms replace them.
