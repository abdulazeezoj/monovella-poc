**Monovella Technologies Limited** (RC2349780), Federal Republic of Nigeria

This copy appears during patient sign-up. Payment authorization appears again

## Before you create your account

Monovella coordinates your specialist consultation and any next steps (a
laboratory test, prescription-based pharmacy order, referral, follow-up,
instruction, or document) inside one Care Journey.

Your care and payments are real. Monovella is not an emergency
service. If your concern may be urgent or an emergency, do not continue through
specialist matching; use the emergency or support route shown on screen.

## Account and service data

Monovella uses your full legal name, date of birth, verified email address,
bookings, payment status, and Care Journey activity to create and secure your
account and provide the service. Anyone with the sign-up link can
sign up; there is no invitation or allowlist check.

`[ ] I agree to Monovella using my identity, contact, booking, payment-status, and service data to create my account and coordinate my Care Journey.`

## Health information

If you continue, Monovella processes health information including a concern you
choose to provide, a note or attachment you share during a live consultation,
consultation notes and original note captures, your Care Plan, documented
prescription or lab request, pharmacy order, and lab result. This is sensitive
personal data.

Only an assigned specialist, assigned laboratory, or selected pharmacy receives
the minimum information needed for its part of your journey. A later Monovella
specialist assigned to you may review prior consultation notes for continuity of
care. A laboratory does not receive SOAP notes, and a pharmacy does not receive
SOAP notes or unrelated results. You can read the full details in the
[Privacy Notice](PRODUCT_PRIVACY.md).

`[ ] I separately consent to Monovella processing my health information to coordinate and document my Care Journey, including continuity-of-care access to prior notes by a later specialist assigned to me.`

Both affirmative actions are required to create an account. Monovella records
the consent text version, scope, and time in the same transaction as account
creation. If either action is missing, no account is created.

## Withdrawing consent

You may withdraw from future consent-based processing at any time through the
account privacy controls or by contacting **dpo@monovella.com**. Withdrawal does
not undo care or payment already completed. Monovella restricts and retains only
records still needed for care continuity, safety, an unresolved transaction or
complaint, or an applicable legal obligation, according to the Privacy Notice.
